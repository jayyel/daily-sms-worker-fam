What this program is

Daily Encouragement Messages is a personal, non-commercial text message program. It sends three short encouragement messages per day to a small number of enrolled family members who have asked to receive them. Each message contains a quotation, a brief grounding suggestion, or a short reflective question. The program sells nothing, advertises nothing, and contains no links.

How people enroll

Enrollment is by direct personal request only. There is no public sign-up form, because this program is not offered to the public. A person joins by asking the program operator directly, in person or by phone, to begin sending the messages, and then confirming from their own mobile phone by texting START to the program number. That confirmation text is the record of consent.

Recipients are told before enrollment how many messages to expect each day, that the messages are automatically generated and scheduled, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can stop the messages at any time by replying STOP.

Message frequency

Three messages per day, sent at approximately 6:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to end all messages immediately. Reply HELP for help, or contact [YOUR EMAIL].

An automated program

Messages are composed in advance and delivered automatically on a schedule. They are not written and sent live by a person at the moment of delivery, and no one monitors replies in real time. Recipients are told this before enrolling.
