Daily Encouragement Messages

A small, private SMS program operated by John Lasak.

What this program is

Daily Encouragement Messages is a personal, non-commercial text message program. It sends three short encouragement messages per day to a small number of enrolled family members who have asked to receive them. Each message contains a quotation, a brief grounding suggestion, or a short reflective question. The program sells nothing, advertises nothing, and contains no links.

How people enroll

A person enrolls by texting the keyword START to the program's number from their own mobile phone. That inbound message is the record of consent and is what activates the subscription. On receiving it, the sender immediately gets a confirmation reply stating the program name, the message frequency, that message and data rates may apply, and how to get help or cancel.

A person may also ask the operator directly to be enrolled, but a spoken request alone never starts messages: they must still text START from their own handset to confirm. Before any enrollment, every person is told the message frequency, that the messages are automatically generated and scheduled in advance, that message and data rates may apply, and that they may stop at any time by replying STOP.

The exact consent language, the auto-replies, and evidence of a completed opt-in are published on our SMS consent and opt-in evidence page.

Message frequency

Three messages per day, sent at approximately 6:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to end all messages immediately. Reply HELP for help, or contact jflasak@gmail.com.

An automated program

Messages are composed in advance and delivered automatically on a schedule. They are not written and sent live by a person at the moment of delivery, and no one monitors replies in real time. Recipients are told this before enrolling.
