This privacy policy applies to the Daily Encouragement Messages SMS program described on our home page. It covers only this messaging program.

Information we collect

We collect only what is required to deliver the messages:

  • The recipient's mobile phone number.
  • The recipient's first name, used to identify their enrollment.
  • Records of consent, including the date consent was given and the confirming text message sent from the recipient's own phone.
  • Message delivery records generated by our messaging provider, such as whether a message was delivered and when.

We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing activity, financial information, or any health information. Recipients are not asked to reply with personal information, and no information is collected from this website.

How we use it

Phone numbers and consent records are used for one purpose only: sending the encouragement messages a person has asked to receive, and honoring their requests for help or to stop. We do not use this information for marketing, advertising, profiling, or analytics.

No sharing or sale of mobile information. We do not share, sell, rent, or trade mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent data with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories of information exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Service providers

We use Twilio Inc. as our messaging provider to transmit the messages. Twilio processes the recipient's phone number and message content solely in order to deliver the message on our behalf, and is bound by its own privacy commitments. Mobile carriers also process messages in order to deliver them. No other party receives this information. We do not share opt-in data with Twilio or any carrier for marketing purposes.

Message frequency and cost

Enrolled recipients receive three messages per day, sent at approximately 6:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Message and data rates may apply.

How long we keep information

We keep a recipient's phone number and consent record for as long as they are enrolled. If a recipient opts out, we stop sending immediately and retain only the minimum record needed to honor that opt-out and to demonstrate compliance.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to end all messages immediately. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] to be removed, to ask what information we hold about you, or to request its deletion.

Children

This program is intended for adults. We do not knowingly enroll anyone under 18 or collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
