SMS Consent and Opt-In Evidence

Daily Encouragement Messages, operated by John Lasak.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This page documents exactly how a person consents to receive messages from the Daily Encouragement Messages program. It exists so that the consent flow can be reviewed publicly, without a login.

Opt-in method 1: keyword opt-in by SMS

Any person may enroll by texting the keyword START to the program's number from their own mobile phone. This inbound message is the record of consent, and it is retained in the program's messaging logs with a timestamp and the originating number.

Upon receiving START, the sender immediately receives this auto-reply:

Daily Encouragement Messages: You are now subscribed to receive 3 encouragement messages per day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Opt-in method 2: spoken request, confirmed by keyword

A person may also ask the program operator directly, in person or by telephone, to be enrolled. When that happens, the operator reads the following disclosure aloud before enrolling anyone:

"This is Daily Encouragement Messages. If you join, you will get three automatically generated text messages a day, at about 6 in the morning, 1:30 in the afternoon, and 6 in the evening. They are scheduled in advance, not written and sent live. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop them at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for help. To confirm that you want them, text the word START to this number from your own phone."

No one is enrolled on a spoken request alone. In every case the person must then text START from their own handset, exactly as in method 1, and that inbound message is what activates the subscription. A spoken request without that confirming text results in no messages being sent.

Opting out

Replying STOP to any message ends the subscription immediately. The sender receives one final confirmation:

Daily Encouragement Messages: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to rejoin.

Getting help

Replying HELP to any message returns:

Daily Encouragement Messages: 3 encouragement messages per day. Contact jflasak@gmail.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.
